Privacy Policy

App name: Flat Map Model: Sun Path 3D · Effective date: November 9, 2026 · 한국어

Summary: This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to anyone.

1. Personal information we collect

None. There is no sign-up or login, and the app never asks for or collects your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.

2. Information handled on your device

The date and time shown in the app are calculated from your device's clock and time zone and are never transmitted. The city and language (Korean/English) you choose from the list are saved only in the app's local storage on your device so they can be reused the next time you open the app, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app. The app does not use GPS or any location service, and these values are never sent anywhere. Other display settings, such as playback speed and view, are not saved.

3. Network access and third parties

The app works without an internet connection and does not communicate with any server. Fonts and the background map are bundled inside the app. It contains no ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and it does not share any information with third parties.

4. Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

5. Permissions

The app does not request any device permissions such as location, camera, microphone, or storage.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the changes and the new effective date on this page.
